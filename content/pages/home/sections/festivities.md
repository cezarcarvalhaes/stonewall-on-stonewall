After the parade, gather at **W 28th + Stonewall** for music, food, and community. Here's some of what you'll find:

- **History of the Stonewall Riots** — read, listen, and learn at the history table.
- **Voter registration** — get registered and ready to make your voice heard.
- **"Yes to All Three"** with Richmond DSA — learn how to support a more equitable Virginia.
- **LGBTQ+ books** from the Richmond Public Library.
- **Free screen printing** with Studio Two Three (4–5PM) — bring anything you'd like to print on.
- **Kid-friendly activities** for the whole family.
- **The Health Brigade** — learn about health services for those in need.
- **Equality Virginia** — learn about the upcoming November referendum.
- **Food trucks** and sweet treats throughout the afternoon.
